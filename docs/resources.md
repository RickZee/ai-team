## Related projects (not this product)

These are useful references. AI-Team stays a **research harness** (three backends + failure taxonomy). We do not clone their distribution models.

| Project | What it is | Why it is not our product |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [ECC](https://github.com/affaan-m/ECC) | Installable skill/plugin overlay for Claude Code, Codex, Cursor, and other IDEs (agents, skills, hooks, AgentShield) | Optimizes the *developer-facing* agent in one workspace. We measure the same nine-role org across three orchestrators. |
| [Tencent teamai-cli](https://github.com/Tencent/teamai-cli) | Git-synced team skills, rules, MCP, hooks, friction-based learnings, recall | Team distribution and knowledge sync. We are a field-study harness, not a team admin CLI. |
| [Stencil Harness Playbook](https://stencil.so/blog/harness-playbook) | Architecture essay: one authoritative session, control plane, bounded work, provider quirks, views as projections | Engine design guidance we borrow constraints from (see [posts/harness-map.md](posts/harness-map.md)). Not a product to fork. |
